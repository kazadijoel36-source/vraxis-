Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2, 2026

1. Introduction

Vraxis Drop ("we," "our," or "the Service") is designed as an ephemeral utility operating from Potchefstroom, South Africa. Our core philosophy is "Send anything. Leave nothing." We prioritize your anonymity and the temporary nature of data.

2. Data Collection & Ephemerality

The Service facilitates the temporary storage of text-based data ("Matter"). By design, all Matter dropped into the system is subject to a Time-To-Live (TTL) constraint. Once the specified time (e.g., 10 minutes, 1 hour, or 24 hours) expires, or the unique access code is utilized for retrieval, the Matter is purged from our active database and is unrecoverable.

3. Advertising & Cookies

To keep Vraxis Drop free for the community, we utilize Google AdSense to serve advertisements. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not require user accounts, email addresses, or personal identity verification. We do not log IP addresses in association with specific "Drops" to maintain the highest level of privacy possible for a public utility.

5. Contact

For inquiries regarding our privacy practices, please contact the Vraxis development team via the Help section.
