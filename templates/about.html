The Origin Note

A quiet move
against the noise.

Vraxis wasn't built in a boardroom. It was built in a dorm room in Potchefstroom, born from a simple realization: the internet has become a series of gates.

Every time you want to share a simple thought, a password, or a draft, you are asked to "Sign In." You are asked to leave a trail. You are asked to trust a database that never forgets.

"I wanted to build a way to send anything, and leave absolutely nothing behind."

As an IT student, I saw how data was being treated—as an asset to be hoarded. Vraxis treats data as Ember. It burns bright for a moment, serves its purpose, and then vanishes. No accounts. No tracking. No logs.

What you see here is a private beta. A quiet channel between devices. It is a one-man attempt to return to a web that was fast, anonymous, and ephemeral.
