ZA-01: The Potch Node.

The "Cloud" is just a marketing term for someone else's computer. For VRAXIS to be truly ephemeral, we had to move away from centralized third-party servers and build our own local infrastructure.

Local Sovereignty

Operating the ZA-01 node from Potchefstroom allows us to maintain a "Zero-Log" routing environment. Most global hosting providers log traffic at the hardware level before it even touches our code. By self-hosting our primary node in the North West, we ensure that your data never touches a corporate logger.

Our current benchmarks show a 38ms average latency across the network, proving that privacy doesn't have to be slow[cite: 2].
