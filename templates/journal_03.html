The Ember Protocol.

Most "Delete" buttons are an illusion. They simply flip a "hidden" flag in a database, keeping the actual data stored on a disk indefinitely. The Ember Protocol is our technical commitment to actual destruction[cite: 3].

Physical Purging

When you retrieve a drop on VRAXIS, we trigger an immediate db.session.delete() command[cite: 3]. This isn't a "soft delete." It is a command to the database engine to unlink and overwrite that specific sector of memory[cite: 3].

Once the 4-digit code is used, the matter is gone. Not just hidden—erased[cite: 3].
